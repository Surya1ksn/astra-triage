# Account: Login and Password Reset Issues

Customers locked out of their account after multiple failed login attempts
are automatically unlocked after 30 minutes, or immediately via a password
reset email.

Steps to help a locked-out customer:

1. Ask the customer to use the "Forgot password" link on the login page.
2. If the reset email doesn't arrive within 5 minutes, check the spam
   folder and confirm the email on file matches what they're checking.
3. If 2FA is enabled and the customer lost access to their authenticator,
   they must go through identity verification before 2FA can be disabled —
   this cannot be done from a support ticket alone.
4. Repeated lockouts from unfamiliar locations may indicate credential
   stuffing; if the customer did not attempt those logins, treat as a
   possible account-security issue and escalate.
