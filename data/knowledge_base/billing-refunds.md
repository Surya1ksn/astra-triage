# Billing: Refunds and Overcharges

If a customer reports being charged twice or an incorrect amount, verify the
last three transactions in the billing dashboard. Refunds for duplicate
charges are auto-approved if requested within 30 days of the charge date.

To process a refund:

1. Confirm the charge ID from the invoice.
2. Check whether a refund was already issued for that charge ID.
3. If the ticket is about a subscription price change, explain that price
   changes take effect at the next renewal date and are communicated by
   email 14 days in advance.
4. Refunds typically post to the original payment method within 5-10
   business days.

Do not issue refunds for charges older than 90 days without escalating to
a billing specialist.
